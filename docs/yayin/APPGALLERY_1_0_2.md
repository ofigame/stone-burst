## English (US) / English (UK)

**Name:** Stone Burst: Puzzle Game

**Brief:** Peel layers, plan moves

**Full:**

Stone Burst is a puzzle game about peeling layered stone stacks.

Tap three or more touching stones of the same color: only their top layer peels off and the colors underneath are revealed. Nothing bursts on its own, every pop is your move. Moves are limited, so plan the right order to collect the goal colors.

What's inside:
• Handcrafted levels, every one verified to be solvable; new colors, more layers and bigger boards as you progress
• Rule levels: exact moves, protect the crystals, scarce colors and more
• Special stones: the Rocket and the Golden Roll
• Ice, vines, locks, envelopes, sponges, rotors, gates and paint
• Relaxing breather levels between the tough ones
• A new daily puzzle every day
• World and country leaderboards
• Collection stones to earn and a Journey map to explore
• Puzzle Performance Index: track how efficiently you solve levels
• Regular levels play without an internet connection

Stone Burst is free to play. It contains optional rewarded ads (extra moves, hints) and ads between levels.

**New features:**
• World and country leaderboards
• Richer, smoother burst effects; lightning takes the color of the stones it hits
• Vines replace clips
• New icon and new backgrounds

(en-GB: "colour", "colours".)
