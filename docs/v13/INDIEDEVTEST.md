# IndieDevTest ilanı (Stone Burst, Google Play kapalı test)

Site: https://indiedevtest.com — ücretsiz, puanla işleyen karşılıklı test topluluğu.
Kural: 2 başlangıç puanı + test edilen her uygulama için 1 puan; 1 puan = 1 kabul edilen testçi.
Hedef: elde 6 kabul eden testçi var, 6 kişi daha (4 uygulama test etmek yeter); düşenlere karşı pay için 8–10.
Kayıt ve giriş kullanıcıda (Claude hesap açmaz). Form alanları giriş yapılmadan görülemedi; aşağıdaki metin alanlara göre uyarlanacak.

## İlan metni (İngilizce)

**App name:** Stone Burst: Puzzle Game

**Category:** Game (Puzzle)

**Short description:**
A relaxing yet clever puzzle game about layers: peel colorful stone layers and trigger chain reactions.

**Description:**
Every tile on the board is a stack of colorful stones, and only the top layer is visible. Tap a group of 3 or more matching stones to peel them away and reveal the colors underneath. When 3 or more stones of the same new color appear together, they burst on their own and set off a chain reaction.

Features: handcrafted levels across several map regions, special stones (rockets and a golden roll), a daily puzzle, a stone collection, leaderboards and offline play in 9 languages.

**How to join:**
1. Join the Google Group: https://groups.google.com/g/stone-burst-testers
2. Open the testing link and tap "Become a tester": https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.ofigame.stoneburst
3. Install Stone Burst from Google Play and keep it installed for the 14-day test.

**What to test / feedback wanted:**
- Does the first level and the rule card make the game clear?
- Any crashes, freezes or slow screens (please mention your device)?
- Are any levels too hard or too easy?
- Does the daily puzzle and the map work as expected?
- Anything confusing in the menus or settings?
