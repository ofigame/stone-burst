```
Stone Burst is a single-player puzzle game. No account or login is needed to play; all levels are playable offline.

HOW TO PLAY
Each tile is a stack of colored stone layers. Tap a group of 3 or more touching stones of the same color to peel the top layer. When the revealed colors form new groups of 3+, they burst on their own (chain reaction). Complete the level goals within the move limit. Levels 1-2 contain a guided tutorial; new mechanics are introduced with a short info card.

HEARTS
From level 21, leaving a level after the first move costs 1 heart (max 5, +1 every 30 minutes). Levels 1-20 never use hearts. When hearts run out, an optional rewarded ad refills them.

ADS
Google AdMob. All rewarded ads are optional: +1 move and one stone placement after running out of moves, a hint, daily puzzle entry, and heart refill. Interstitial ads appear only after level 11, at most every 4 levels and at least 4 minutes apart. In the EU the Google consent form is shown; Settings contains a "Privacy options" row.

GAME CENTER / ONLINE
Game Center sign-in is optional and used for the leaderboard and cloud save of progress (Firebase). Declining sign-in does not limit gameplay.

There are no in-app purchases.
```
