This Privacy Policy explains what data the mobile games developed by {{company}} (“we”, “the games”, for example foi | cell and Stone Burst) and the {{site}} website process, why, and how.

1. Data we collect ourselves

Our games do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts or precise location and never ask for a password. Your progress, settings and in-game items (coins, outfits, etc.) are stored only on your device, unless you choose the cloud save described below.

Online profile (foi | cell): when you first open the game you are asked to pick a username; when you use the online features (the Challenge duel, friends) an online profile is created on our servers. It holds: a player ID and secret key generated at random on your device (only a hashed digest of the key is stored), the username you choose, your trophies and rank, your foi's weapon and armour level, your army's upgrade points, the results of the matches you play, your friends list and friend requests. Your username, rank and trophies are visible to other players (as a rival in duels, in friends lists and on the leaderboard), so do not use your real name or other personal information as your username. Usernames are checked against offensive words and can be changed once a week. In live duels, the position and blows of your foi and its soldiers are relayed through our server to your rival only and are not stored; only the match result is recorded. Duel invitations can only be sent to your friends and are deleted automatically shortly after. If you allow notifications, your device's notification token is stored so we can notify you of duel invitations and friend requests; notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. When no rival is found, some matches are played against computer-controlled players; they are not real people. This data is used only to run the online features (matchmaking, match results, leaderboard, friends and invitations) and to prevent cheating; it is not used for advertising and is not sold. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). You can delete your profile at any time in the game under Settings → Delete my online profile (see Data Deletion).

Game account and leaderboard (Stone Burst): Stone Burst signs you in with Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android). From that service we receive only your player ID and your Game Center / Play Games nickname; we never see your Apple or Google password or e-mail address. When you reach the leaderboard, the following is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore) so it can be shown on the world and country leaderboards: your nickname, your leaderboard score, the level you have reached, your stars, your in-game performance index (ZPE, a game statistic, not an IQ test) and your country code, which is taken from your device's region setting (not from your location). Your nickname, score and country are visible to other players on the leaderboard. If you tap Save progress, a copy of your game progress is stored in your Game Center / Google Play Games account storage or on Google Firebase, so you can restore it on a new device. This data is used only to run the leaderboard and cloud save; it is not used for advertising and is not sold. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS).

If you use the contact form on our website, the name, e-mail address and message you send are stored only to reply to you.

2. Advertising

Our games are free and supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob and by the ad networks that work through AdMob mediation. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud these services may collect:

  • the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA – only if you allow it),
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it (country/city level),
  • technical information such as device model, operating system, language and app version,
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, completed rewarded ads).

This processing is governed by each company's own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

See Advertising & Ad Partners for details.

3. Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent (UMP) form on first launch. You can accept or refuse personalised ads and change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the game.
  • On iOS, cross-app tracking happens only if you allow it in Apple's “App Tracking Transparency” prompt.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Users covered by US state laws (such as CCPA/CPRA) may opt out of the “sale/sharing” of personal information through their device settings and the in-game privacy options.

4. Legal basis

Personalised ads rely on your consent. Non-personalised ads, game security and fraud prevention rely on our legitimate interests; running the leaderboard and cloud save relies on providing the game features you use; answering your support requests relies on fulfilling your request (KVKK art. 5 and GDPR art. 6).

5. Children

Our games are intended for a general audience and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or a higher age where required by local law). If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Retention and security

We keep support messages for at most 2 years after your request is closed. Your foi | cell online profile is kept until you delete it; deleting it removes the profile and all your match records immediately and permanently. Your Stone Burst leaderboard entry and cloud save are kept while you play; to delete them, write to {{email}} with your in-game nickname and we will delete them within 30 days. We protect data with reasonable technical and organisational measures.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and object to the processing of your data. Send requests to {{email}} or through our support form. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).
